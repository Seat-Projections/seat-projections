Read before quoting

  • The ranges are too narrow. Across five state election backtests, the Forecast Model’s 80% ranges for the final two candidates contained the actual vote share about 59% of the time when it picked the correct final pair. Allow for more uncertainty than the ranges suggest.
  • One Nation is harder to estimate. Its local support and preference flows rely on assumptions.
