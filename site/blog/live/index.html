Updates

Updated

9:34pm

With about 23% of votes counted, the ABC has now called 49 seats: Coalition 39 (Liberal 30, Nationals 9), Greens 7 and Labor 3. Another 39 are still in doubt.

Five seats from our last update are back in doubt, including Geelong, our only miss at that point, and Melton, which the ABC had called for One Nation. They're out of the tally until the ABC calls them again.

Our forecast picked the winning party in 47 of the 49 called seats. The ABC has called Bendigo West for the Nationals, where we favoured Labor (63%). It has called St Albans for Labor, where we narrowly favoured the Liberals (55%).

Point Cook, now called for the Liberals, was one of our closest seats. We had it as a near coin flip between the Liberals and One Nation, with the Liberals fractionally ahead.

8:47pm

Results so far, based on the 48 seats the ABC has called with about 15% of votes counted: Coalition 38 (Liberal 31, Nationals 7), Greens 7, Labor 2 and One Nation 1. Another 40 seats are still in doubt.

Our final forecast picked the winning party in 47 of those 48 seats. The one miss so far is Geelong. We had it as a four-way contest with Labor narrowly favoured (39%), ahead of the Liberals (28%). The ABC has called it for the Liberals.

Our favourites' win chances in these seats imply about seven misses, so the forecast is doing better than expected so far. The early calls are mostly the clearer contests, though. The real test is the 40 seats still in doubt.
