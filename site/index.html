Read before quoting

  • The ranges are too narrow. Across five state election backtests, the Forecast Model’s 80% ranges for the final two candidates contained the actual vote share about 59% of the time when it picked the correct final pair. Allow for more uncertainty than the ranges suggest. Small differences in win chance are not meaningful.
  • One Nation is harder to estimate. Its local support and preference flows rely on assumptions. The model does not yet use registered how-to-vote cards.
  • The views answer different questions. The switch offers two views: the Forecast Model, which combines current polls with the 2022 state result and the declared 2026 candidates, and the Baseline Model, which uses 2025 federal support without a polling adjustment. A federal-baseline variant of the Forecast Model is also available for comparison through the methodology page.
