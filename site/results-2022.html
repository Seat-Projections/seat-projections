What the 2022 result tells us going into 2026

Labor holds 56 of 88 seats on 36.7% of the statewide first-preference vote — a second successive term won on 2CP margins rather than primaries. The Coalition holds 28 (19 Liberal, 9 Nationals) on a combined 34.5% primary, and the Greens hold four inner-Melbourne seats on 11.5%.

The closest seats in 2022 were Northcote (0.2%), Bass (0.2%) and Pakenham (0.4%). Twelve seats were decided by under 5% on the final two-candidate count — the belt where the 2026 election is most likely to be decided. No independents were elected, despite 5.6% of the statewide first-preference vote.

One Nation polled just 0.3% of the Lower House primary in 2022; any 2026 presence of the kind the federal results suggest is therefore mostly invisible in these results — the reason the forecast models it from federal booth patterns rather than these results.
