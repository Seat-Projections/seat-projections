Read before quoting

  • Intervals are overconfident. On the replay benchmarks, the models’ 80% 2CP ranges contained the actual result only about 44% of the time — treat p10–p90 ranges as minimum uncertainty, and small probability differences as noise.
  • One Nation is partly estimated. Its geographic pattern and preference flows are placeholder/estimated values; registered how-to-vote cards are not yet used.
  • Two different questions. Model 2 forecasts the declared 2026 candidates on the polling average; Model 1 answers “what if the 2025 federal result were replayed with no swing” and uses no polling at all.
